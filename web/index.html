Controls and rules

Play: click your piece, then a green destination. Choose a promotion when offered, or Cancel to keep the position. White moves first. New game resets the shared match; refreshing this page reads the existing match. Geometry sandbox inspects fixed positions without changing the game.

3D: drag to orbit, right-drag or Shift-drag to pan, wheel to zoom. Focus the canvas: arrows orbit, WASD pan, +/− zoom, R reset. 2D: click a square. Changing view, slice or piece style preserves selection and never moves a piece. Sprites are the default; choose Text if an image fails to load.

The arena starts with 48 pieces. Bishops follow plane diagonals, excluding body diagonals. Pawns advance along rank or tier separately. Orange/gold marks selection; green marks Faber-supplied destinations. A selection outside a slice is reported beneath it.
